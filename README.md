I'm a Biomedical Data Scientist with a background in biology, pharmaceutical manufacturing, drug safety, and healthcare analytics. I enjoy using data to answer meaningful questions, uncover patterns, and build solutions that can improve healthcare and patient outcomes.

One of the things I appreciate most about my career is that it hasn't followed a traditional path. Each role has given me a different perspective on healthcare and the pharmaceutical industry, and every experience has shown me how much of an impact data can have when it's used thoughtfully.

Over time, I realized that the work I enjoyed most always came back to asking questions, finding patterns, and using information to support better decisions. That realization led me to data science, where I can bring together my scientific background, healthcare experience, and curiosity to solve problems that matter.

When I'm not building models or exploring datasets, I'm usually learning something new, whether that's a machine learning technique or a new recipe to try. Every once in a while, though, I'm probably treating myself to a little retail therapy that starts with, *"I'm just here for one thing,"* and somehow ends with a basket full.
